The frozen definitive public-source search covered 1 January 2000 to 10 September 2026 across PubMed, Europe PMC and arXiv. It retrieved 11,933 source--query records and produced 6,358 identifier- or title-deduplicated candidates; 6,354 non-quarantined records entered the human title-and-abstract screening ledger, with 1,747 high- or possible-signal records used only to order screening. All 84 configured source--query pairs completed, no result-cap truncation remained, and returned counts matched source-reported totals. OpenAlex remains a supplementary discovery source rather than part of this definitive count because its broad search parameter has not been validated as an equivalent Boolean bibliographic search. The strategy retrieved 21/24 representative manuscript seeds and 17/17 seeds coded as direct, bounded, human-mediated or assisted-sampling evidence. These are retrieval and workload denominators, not included-study counts or estimates of field size. Duplicate independent screening, full-text exclusion records, report--study--system linkage, citation chasing and design-specific risk-of-bias assessment remain required before any systematic-review claim.
